# Payroll and HR replacement: evidence review (20 October 2026)

**Organization:** 120-employee construction contractor; five or more payroll states; field/mobile hours; benefits; onboarding; **QuickBooks Enterprise Desktop**; target deployment within six months.

**Decision rule:** A supplier is not fully qualified until its exact QuickBooks Enterprise edition/version interface and committed deployment date are demonstrated. Vendor descriptions and online-accounting integrations are not such proof.

### Normalized total-cost model (NOT vendor quotes or observed market prices)

For transparent budgeting, use an **illustrative sensitivity range** of **$18–$35/employee/month** for the integrated suite, **$5,000–$20,000** one-time onboarding, **$2,000–$12,000** one-time accounting interface/configuration, and **$0–$6,000/year** integration support. None is a supplier quotation or verified forecast. The same assumptions apply to *every* candidate to avoid inventing a false price ranking.

- At **120 staff**, core subscription is **$25,920–$50,400/year** (120 × 12 × $18–$35).
- Illustrative first-year all-in sensitivity: **$32,920–$88,400** (core + one-time onboarding + interface + annual interface support).
- Illustrative three-year sensitivity, holding headcount/prices constant: **$84,760–$201,200** (3 × [core + annual support] + one-time fees).

The wide range is **not evidence of any specific vendor's likely price**. RFP quotes must separately show time and attendance, benefits/carrier feeds, certified-payroll tooling, overtime/union rates, five-state taxes, GL jobs/classes, payroll cycles, support tiers, migration, training, year-two escalation, minimums, add-ons, and termination/export costs.

### Six-month feasibility test

Require a dated vendor plan for 24 weeks: weeks 1–3 discovery/data audit; 4–8 migration and five-state tax setup; 9–13 job-cost/time/benefits configuration; 14–17 Enterprise interface test and exception handling; 18–20 at least two full parallel payrolls including overtime and multiple states; 21–24 cutover, training, reconciliations and contingency. This is a **proposed acceptance schedule**, not a current supplier commitment. Stop purchasing if the provider cannot reserve implementation specialists and commit to the gate dates in writing.

## Outcome: no winning platform demonstrated

**No single recommendation.** The evidence does not establish one fully-qualified supplier under the mandatory QuickBooks Enterprise Desktop and implementation constraints. This is an **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** procurement outcome rather than an assertion that the products perform equally.

**Most important unresolved differences:**

1. **Enterprise compatibility:** ADP + Dapt publishes a QuickBooks Desktop route, not a signed demonstration for the precise Enterprise version/edition; Paylocity, Paycor and UKG have not substantiated an equivalent Enterprise interface. Paychex explicitly disclaims Desktop integration.
2. **Accounting depth:** For job/class/cost-code allocations, certified payroll, overtime and multi-jurisdiction pay treatment, ADP documents greater domain specificity. Actual report contents, audit trail and integration error management still need a scripted demonstration.
3. **Field operation:** Paylocity has stronger general-user mobile/support review scores. ADP has geofenced job-site clocking. Neither review scores nor marketing proves reliable offline capture and crew-to-job transfers at this client's job sites.
4. **Cost and support:** No executable proposals for 120 staff, five state footprints, support SLAs or QuickBooks mapping were obtained. Vendor pricing cannot be ranked responsibly.
5. **Delivery risk:** A generalized six-month implementation range and 'fast implementation' marketing are not an agreed payroll parallel-test and go-live schedule.

**Procurement action, not a purchase recommendation:** Request identical written scopes and 36-month total-cost bids from ADP (including Dapt), Paylocity and UKG Ready, with Paycor as an additional bidder where feasible. Prioritize a **technical proof-of-concept** with ADP's Desktop pathway, not a software award. The vendor must execute a sample five-state payroll and export a balancing journal, with per-job and per-cost-code transactions, into a copy of the **actual QuickBooks Enterprise Desktop** company file. Verify taxes, deductions, benefits, overtime, off-cycle payments, reimports, reversal/error logs and reconciliation to both systems. Require a six-month committed go-live schedule and service-level details.

A single winner is authorized **only after** a candidate passes every mandatory demonstration and shows a material, documented advantage in the six comparison dimensions. Until then, maintain the current systems, continue the RFP, and do not issue an unconditional award.

## Evidence provenance and remaining procurement limits

Research used vendor feature/marketplace pages, vendor implementation/pricing pages and aggregated G2 comparisons accessed 20 October 2026. No contract-specific product demonstrations, quotations, audited support logs, five-state payroll records or payroll implementation plans were supplied. G2 results are general user experiences and must not be interpreted as controlled comparisons for construction jobsites. The Desktop-compatible third-party accounting module adds a vendor coordination point; it is not the same thing as a single-vendor end-to-end accounting interface.

**Final analytical state: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.** Do not select a winner.
